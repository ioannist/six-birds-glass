This note repairs the finite mathematical bridge without editing the paper.
It does not certify the existing filing's other gates. In particular, future
separation and fixed points of a packaging operator are different obligations.

\paragraph{Declared carrier and legal preparations.}
Let $X=\{0,1\}^8$, $e(x)=\sum_i x_i$, and $K$ be the random-site, wall-up East
heat-bath kernel at $\epsilon=2/5$. Its stationary law is the product law with
occupation probability $\epsilon/(1+\epsilon)$. The frozen configuration
\texttt{configs/gt6\_shell\_witness\_n8.json} prescribes initial equilibrium
at $7/10$, twenty quench steps at $1/10$, and a thirty-step observation window
at $2/5$. Define
\[
 \mu_t=\pi_{7/10} K_{1/10}^{20} K^t,\qquad 0\leq t\leq9,
 \qquad a_t(j)=\mu_t\{e=j\},\quad 0\leq j\leq8.
\]
A legal preparation may choose a stopping time independently of the spin
trajectory, using a precomputed probability law on these ten times, then run
the stated kernel for that number of steps. Subsequent experiments begin from
that stopped spin law. This extension of the deterministic-run carrier must
be declared: the two sources are convex random stopping mixtures, rather
than two deterministic stopping times or path-dependent selection rules.

\paragraph{Current-only selector.}
Let $A$ have columns $a_0,\ldots,a_9$. Rational RREF, with the first free
column assigned coefficient one and subsequent free coefficients zero,
produces a nonzero $c\in\ker A$. For the frozen data, the rank is nine and
the free column is nine. Because each column has mass one,
$\sum_t c_t=0$. Therefore
\[
 z=\sum_t(c_t)_+=\sum_t(-c_t)_+>0,\qquad
 p_t=(c_t)_+/z,\quad q_t=(-c_t)_+/z
\]
are probability weights. Define $h_+=\sum_t p_t\mu_t$ and
$h_-=\sum_t q_t\mu_t$. They are nonnegative probability laws and
\[
 a(h_+)-a(h_-)=Ac/z=0.
\]
Only current energy-law columns enter this selector. Neither future energy
values nor an equilibrium target enter it. A null vector alone does not
guarantee a future split: stationary repeated columns yield identical
mixtures, and the implementation rejects such a false target.

\paragraph{Strictness and its exact computational premises.}
Freeze the selector, then evaluate $u_\tau(h)=hK^\tau e$ for every
$\tau\in H=\{1,\ldots,21\}$. All preparations and continuations remain
inside the declared window because $9+21=30$. Exact rational replay shows
\[
 d_\tau=|u_\tau(h_+)-u_\tau(h_-)|>0\quad(\tau\in H),
 \qquad d=\min_{\tau\in H}d_\tau>0.
\]
The machine-readable certificate stores the rational values, coefficients,
weights, current columns, source-law hashes and binding configuration.
The minimum is approximately $1.486528865476599\cdot10^{-10}$; this decimal
is illustrative, not the proof of positivity.

On the declared two-object carrier set
\[
 \pi_0(h)=a(h),\qquad
 \pi_1(h)=(a(h),(u_\tau(h))_{\tau\in H}).
\]
The projection to the first coordinate retains $\pi_0$. If a function
$f$ satisfied $\pi_1=f\circ\pi_0$, equality of the current vectors would
force equality of every future coordinate, contradicting any $d_\tau>0$.
Thus the predictive layer strictly extends the \emph{complete current energy
law}, not merely its mean. This is the split-pair-to-nonfactorization direction
of F-III T12/T13. T19/T20 concern consequences of accepted verdicts; they
cannot replace proofs of the other gates. On this finite carrier all
$2^9=512$ energy predicates also agree, as certified by exhaustive exact
summing. No claim about arbitrary other carriers follows from this example.

\paragraph{Finite persistence and quantitative robustness.}
As a corollary of full current-law equality, every descriptor computed from
that law, including nonlinear summaries and any collection of energy-only
scalars, takes the same value on the two histories. Such a descriptor cannot
be future-sufficient. The corresponding generic and actual-table corollaries
are mechanized in Lean. In the declared scalar buyback pool the expected
grid-index fictive-temperature readout $T_f$ indeed fails to separate the
pair. The bond-count mean and first-spin mean do separate it, so either
restores zero loss on this two-history exhibit. The certificate records all
subset losses, using the sum of two unit-weight squared errors, not their
mean. MaxFiber is two on this current fiber; this does not lower-bound the
number of real-valued scalar coordinates needed to resolve arbitrary memory.

For probability measures $\nu,\eta$ and a bounded observable with range
$[0,8]$, $|\nu e-\eta e|\leq8\operatorname{TV}(\nu,\eta)$.
Consequently the future energy laws have TV distance at least $d_\tau/8$.
Markov kernels contract TV. If each source law is perturbed by at most
$r=d/(4\cdot8)$, then each perturbed future gap is at least
$d-2\cdot8r=d/2>0$ throughout $H$. To retain the nonfactorization conclusion,
the perturbed sources must additionally have equal current energy laws;
arbitrary perturbations do not preserve that equality. This is a finite
window result, with a tiny but explicit tolerance. It is not a claim of
macroscopic gap size, experimental detectability, all-time persistence,
asymptotic metastability, or a window-uniform large-$N$ theorem.

\paragraph{Target-coupled negative control.}
Keep $h_+,h_-$ and their common current vector unchanged, and replace only
the future probe kernel by unconstrained random-site heat bath at $2/5$.
Its energy process is lumpable: at energy $j$, the probabilities of gaining
and losing one spin are $(8-j)(2/7)/8$ and $j(5/7)/8$, respectively.
Equal initial energy laws therefore give equal future energy laws at every
time. Exact replay checks that every mean-energy gap on $H$ is zero.
This ablation changes the actual experiment, rather than deleting a required
field from a conjunction.

\paragraph{Packaging obstruction.}
Let $Q$ map a law to its energy law, and $U$ relift each energy level using
the conditional Gibbs prototype (uniform within that level). The row-kernel
implementation of $E$ first evolves by $K$, then applies $Q$, then $U$.
The exact graph check shows it is irreducible; exact arithmetic shows
$\pi_{2/5}E=\pi_{2/5}$. A finite irreducible stochastic kernel has a unique
stationary probability law. Hence this $E$ cannot possess multiple exact
fixed-point strata of probability laws. Its maximum prototype retention
error at one step is $5/7$, not a small number. Indeed the all-up prototype
leaves energy eight with probability $5/7$, while any spin-flip probability
is at most $5/7$. Saturation of a set of support labels does not certify
fixed points of $E$. Finite predictive persistence therefore cannot be
used to mark a completion-fixed-point stability gate as passed.
