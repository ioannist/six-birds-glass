\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{What is established, at which grade}

\emph{Theorem-grade}, each on its exactly declared finite class: the energy readout is not closed,
with a margin over the nonzero equilibrium baseline certified in exact rational arithmetic (GT1);
the Kovacs split-pair witness (GT2); foreclosure of all $512$ energy-definable predicates, with a
finer present-state readout as the exhibited separator (GT3); the kill-test panel, with five
look-alike regimes rejected (GT4); $\mathrm{MaxFiber}=2$ with a saturating buyback ledger (GT5);
the limiting-pressure bounds and the zero conditional limit on the GT6 shell; and, from the
formalization, the collapse theorem for the legacy core (machine-checked).
\emph{Certificate-grade, shell-local}: GT6's nonfactorization of the predictive description through
the complete current energy law, over a $21$-horizon window. \emph{Run-only}: the GT7 protocol and
its 8/14/294/22 scoreboard. \emph{Recognition-grade}: the GB1 and GB2 demonstrations, the Lean
fidelity filing, and the three empirical bridges. Nothing in this paper is graded higher than its
artifact.

Taken together, the witnesses form a ladder. GT2 matches the mean energy of a Kovacs crossing
against equilibrium. GT3 matches the complete energy law on a constructed reflection pair,
separated by a present structural readout under the identity continuation. GT6 matches the
complete energy law for two preparations of one aging run and lets the East dynamics separate
them over $21$ horizons. Each rung removes one way of saying ``you just did not look at the energy
carefully enough.'' In every case the separating information is the arrangement of up spins,
which the energy does not record; in GT2 and GT6 it is the East constraint that turns that
arrangement into a different future.

\subsection{What is open}

Each open item has a register entry stating what closing it would take
(\texttt{paper/gap\_register.md}). None is load-bearing for a claim above, because every claim's
wording already excludes it.

\claimref{NEG.gb5\_repair\_sweep\_not\_built}{5a52aef79c46179740f51304a896ff2f2c5ff635930d00bc77fca3dfdf7c3a37}
The largest is GB5: no exhaustive sweep over a frozen class of candidate repairs exists, so no
repair-impossibility certificate of any grade is claimed. ``No admissible repair exists'' is never
asserted, and GT5's buyback saturation is explicitly not a substitute.

The others:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{GT6's full filing.} The fixed-point stability gate fails on this shell because the
    packaging kernel has a unique stationary law. Restoring the full filing would need a
    different completion or limit construction, or a declared theory of approximate finite-time
    strata with its own tolerances.
  \item \textbf{A positive conditional pressure gap.} Proved impossible in the infinite-time limit
    for initial-law conditioning in this finite primitive model; the finite-time and killed-path
    contrasts are different objects.
  \item \textbf{Full-loop lawfulness.} The knockout panel is a diagnostic survey; a causal
    six-primitive ablation needs a common substrate and genuine mechanisms.
  \item \textbf{The GT2 loop certificate}, unfiled because the real loop's history family does not
    close within six rounds.
  \item \textbf{GT1's [B]-parity control} (skipped, filed) and \textbf{GT1's breadth}: one declared
    (model, $N$, lens) cell, which is the declared-class discipline working as designed, but
    honestly a single cell.
  \item \textbf{GT7}: the interpretation of the failures (three readings left open by design), the
    absence of per-readout witnesses behind ``run-only'', and the absence of retained evidence for
    the chronological order of the freeze stages.
  \item \textbf{GB1's transient regime}: the corrected budget is stationary only.
  \item \textbf{Material forcing}, absent on this shell, so [C]'s fuller route is unexercised.
  \item \textbf{Bridge thresholds} marked \texttt{pending\_source}.
\end{itemize}

Natural next experiments follow directly: a second registered prediction round with re-derived
intervals and dated freezes; an Arrhenius-form trap family (per-trap rather than common
temperature dependence) so that the Kovacs protocol is non-degenerate there; the FA,
$L_{\mathrm{panel}}$ and $N{=}10$ extensions of GT1; a full-energy-law witness at larger $N$ or with
larger gaps; and laboratory matched pairs built by randomized stopping.

One ceiling is declared rather than open, per the framework's recognition-open caveat: if glass
aging carries a finite conservation or balance law over the declared records that supplies none of
the six witness types, the six-role decomposition underlying this paper's filings is conditional
on the recognition hypothesis that no such feature exists. No such candidate was identified on the
audited class; any future one files at recognition grade, separately.

\subsection{Anti-reductionism, stated plainly}

This paper's certificates live at a boundary between two levels of description, the
present-observable level and the predictive level, and the discipline that produced them cuts both
ways. A passing certificate suite never proves emergence, and none of the information-theoretic
emergence criteria in the literature \cite{hoel-2013,rosas-2020} is invoked. GT3 and GT6 say that
the declared energy description provably cannot express the distinction that memory carries;
that is the method working as intended, not a metaphysical discovery about glass, and not a
reduction of either level to the other. Symmetrically, nothing here licenses reading the
strictness of the memory level as autonomy, drive or an arrow of time: no P6\_drive audit was
performed, and a positive closure deficit is neither novelty nor irreversibility on its own. The
paper makes no claim about the existence or non-existence of a thermodynamic ideal-glass
transition, takes no side in the field's thermodynamic-versus-dynamical debate
\cite{biroli-garrahan-2013}, and makes no continuum-limit claims. Every quantifier in every claim
ranges over a declared finite class, and the empirical legs are bridge claims only. The complete
nonclaims ledger, every nonclaim of every claim row, unabridged, is printed in
Appendix~\ref{app:nonclaims}.
