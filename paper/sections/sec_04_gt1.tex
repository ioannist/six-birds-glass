\section{GT1: The Energy Readout Is Not Closed}
\label{sec:gt1}

\subsection{Claim}

In plain terms: for an aging East chain, the microstate tells you substantially more about the
energy a few steps ahead than the present energy does, and it does so by a certified factor more
than at constrained equilibrium. Precisely:

\claimref{GT1}{287c64d67c666ebac399a10b237a8738183e25f41de1406d18153549dbbeadab}
On the declared scope (East model, $N=8$, lens $L_{\mathrm{energy}}$, horizon ladder
$\tau \in \{1,2,4,8\}$, quench $\epsilon: 7/10 \to 1/10$ with waiting time $t_w = 20$), the
closure deficit $\mathrm{CD}_\tau$ of the aging protocol exceeds the constrained-East
stationary-equilibrium baseline at every declared horizon by at least the declared margin $3/2$.
The certified lower bound on the smallest ratio is $1.6391$, at $\tau=8$
(Table~\ref{tab:gt1-margins}). Every inequality is proved in exact arithmetic: the joint and
conditional probabilities are exact rationals and each logarithm is enclosed between
outward-rounded rational bounds, so the decimal values shown are illustrative. Alongside, the
one-step idempotence defect of the packaging map is $1225/1936$ for the cold ($\epsilon=1/10$)
quench kernel and $1225/4624$ for the hot ($\epsilon=7/10$) equilibrium kernel, an exact ratio
$289/121$ above its declared margin $2$. Grade: theorem (audited class), exact rational. In the
framework's filing vocabulary: the P5 channel of the standard thermodynamic packaging is not
\texttt{active\_projection} as a closed packaging on this scope; packaging the present energy
readout does not yield a closed description. Anchors: F-III T8, F-I D-IC-01/D-IC-02/T-CL-01,
\sbtcite{sbt-paper-b} Def.~3.1.

The two packaging defects are each bounded by a different, larger quantity, the

\subsection{The controls, and why equilibrium is not zero}

The controls deserve emphasis, because the intuitive control is wrong. One might expect an
equilibrated chain to have nothing left to remember, and so to serve as a zero-deficit control.
That expectation is \emph{false}, and provably so. $\mathrm{CD}_\tau = 0$ for a fully supported
distribution if and only if the energy readout is $\tau$-lumpable, and lumpability depends on
the kinetic constraint, not on stationarity. In East a spin can flip only when its left neighbor
is up, so the number of spins free to move, and hence the rate at which the energy can change,
depends on \emph{where} the up spins sit, not merely on how many there are. The energy readout of
East is therefore not closed even at equilibrium.

The certificate uses two controls with different jobs (Fig.~\ref{fig:gt1-ladder}). The
\emph{unconstrained} model, whose energy readout is lumpable by construction, sits at exact
rational $\mathrm{CD} = 0$ (exact lumpability verified symbolically; float evaluation gives
residuals near $10^{-17}$). Constrained-East equilibrium is an honest \emph{nonzero baseline}. The
content of the certificate is that aging exceeds even that baseline by the declared margin.
Equilibrium is never used as a zero control.

\claimref{GB2}{847f389464f9196f2315c05e3cef842efa94f201aad26f19b5dc4071e043f081}
Because the aging chain is driven by a temperature schedule, the closure deficit must be defined
relative to the protocol rather than to a single stationary kernel. GB2 supplies that machinery:
finite protocol-relative deficit computations (East versus unconstrained, $N=4$, declared
$\epsilon$ grid), with exactness reserved for the lumpability check and float64 windowed
diagnostics labeled as such. GB2 is recognition-grade, a demonstration rather than a certificate.
The GT1 artifact carries a passing consistency gate for it: on a single stationary cell the
protocol-relative deficit equals the stationary deficit by definition, with residual exactly $0$.

\subsection{Scope and an open control}

The certificate quantifies over exactly its declared cell: East, $N=8$, $L_{\mathrm{energy}}$,
the declared horizon ladder and the declared quench. FA replication, the richer
$L_{\mathrm{panel}}$ lens and $N=10$ are extensions that were not run, listed as such in the
artifact envelope.

\claimref{NEG.gt1\_b\_parity\_control\_open}{287c64d67c666ebac399a10b237a8738183e25f41de1406d18153549dbbeadab}
One intended control is open: the benchmark-parity check against the example chains of paper [B]
was not ported, and the GT1 artifact files it as an explicitly skipped control with its reason
(machine-readable \texttt{status: skipped}). The margins are internal to the declared scope and do
not route through [B]'s example chains; no [B]-parity is claimed until the control is run.
