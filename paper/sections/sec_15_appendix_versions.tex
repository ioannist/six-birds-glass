\section{Version History}
\label{app:versions}

\paragraph{v1 (23 September 2026).} First release, DOI \href{https://doi.org/\paperfirstdoi}{\paperfirstdoi}.

\paragraph{v2 (3 October 2026).} This version, DOI \href{https://doi.org/\paperdoi}{\paperdoi}. An independent mathematical and Lean audit of every certificate,
with the following consequences.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{GT6.} The strict-extension result is now the nonfactorization of the predictive
    description through the complete current energy law, on a new same-shell witness built from
    randomized stops (\S\ref{sec:gt6}). The fuller filing's fixed-point stability gate is
    reported as failed, with a proof that the packaging kernel has a unique stationary law. The
    v1 verdict basis, which cited off-shell witnesses through verdict-level theorems, is
    withdrawn. The pressure diagnostic is replaced by certified limit bounds, a proof that the
    infinite-time conditional gap is zero, and separately labeled finite-time and killed-path
    contrasts.
  \item \textbf{GT1.} The margin is certified with exact probabilities and rational logarithm
    enclosures (v1: deterministic float64). The scope lists only the temperatures actually used,
    and the retention error is computed as its own quantity rather than copied from the
    idempotence defect.
  \item \textbf{Lean.} The numeric witnesses of GT2 and GT6 are mechanized as exact lookup tables
    in a declared-event core; v1 had no numeric witness in Lean. The fidelity table is generated
    from the live build (97 rows; v1: 17). The legacy collapse finding now includes explicit
    loop-asymmetry impossibility.
  \item \textbf{GB1.} The uncorrected stationary candidate bound is refuted by an exact
    counterexample and replaced by a proved budget with a contraction residual; the three-state
    demonstration is labeled float64 (v1 called it exact).
  \item \textbf{GT4.} The cleared protocol-trap and flattenable fixtures now preserve futures and
    support respectively, instead of erasing futures with a constant reset.
  \item \textbf{GT2, GT5, GT7, GB10.} The loop certificate's absence is explained by the real
    loop's non-closure; the GT5 loss is stated as a sum of squared errors; GT7 states what content
    hashes do and do not establish about chronology, and that scored transfer covers hump readouts
    only; bridge fields pending source verification are flagged.
  \item The exposition was rewritten throughout, with a new abstract, a results overview, a
    witness diagram and a figure for the GT6 witness. The GT7 scoreboard, its frozen registries
    and its scorer are unchanged.
\end{itemize}
